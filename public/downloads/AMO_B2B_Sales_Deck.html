OEM / ODM Process

From idea to levitation. From first brief to commercial delivery.

01 Brief

We define the product idea, target market, application scenario, and customization depth.

Inputs

Object type, use case, quantity range, budget direction, timeline, and brand requirements.

02 Concept Design

We translate the brief into floating product directions, form language, and presentation logic.

Outputs

Visual direction, object-to-base proportion, material mood, brand placement, and application fit.

03 Engineering

We develop the levitation architecture around weight, balance, rotation, electronics, and long-term stability.

Focus

Magnetic system, mechanical structure, control electronics, load capacity, rotation behavior, and safety.

04 Prototype

We build working prototypes to validate levitation behavior, balance, lighting, and overall presentation quality.

Validation

Floating stability, rotation rhythm, visual effect, object balance, and user experience.

05 Sampling

We refine pre-production samples around finish, packaging, assembly detail, and client review.

Review

Surface finish, branding, packaging direction, reliability checks, and commercial readiness.

06 Mass Production

We lock the production process around repeatability, inspection standards, and delivery planning.

Production

Component sourcing, assembly process, quality inspection, packaging, and production scheduling.

07 Global Delivery

We support export-ready delivery for distributors, design studios, brand teams, and launch programs.

Support

Export coordination, branded packing, documentation, and rollout planning.

Engineering Lens

The strongest project outcomes happen when levitation architecture is considered during concept planning, not after the form is frozen.

Lead Time / MOQ Logic

Lead time and MOQ depend on customization depth, engineering risk, object complexity, and production readiness.

01 Reference / Standard Product Adaptation

Best For

Existing AMO product directions with logo, finish, color, packaging, or light customization.

Typical Path

Fastest route to sample review and small-batch production.

MOQ Logic

Lower MOQ may be possible when the structure and magnetic architecture are already validated.

02 Semi-custom OEM Project

Best For

Custom base form, object shell, material direction, branding, packaging, and application-specific presentation.

Typical Path

Concept design -> engineering review -> working prototype -> sample approval -> production.

MOQ Logic

MOQ depends on tooling, material, electronics, assembly complexity, and packaging requirements.

03 Full ODM Development

Best For

New product lines, custom floating objects, dedicated magnetic architecture, and private-label programs.

Typical Path

Brief -> concept -> engineering -> prototype -> sample -> production planning -> rollout.

MOQ Logic

Higher MOQ may apply when new tooling, custom electronics, or dedicated production setup is required.

Reference Logic

Existing and semi-custom programs may use 45-60 days after sample approval as a reference pattern. Final timing still depends on object, customization, quantity, and delivery scope.

Timeline Guidance

Initial discussion: 1-3 days

Concept direction: 5-10 business days

Prototype planning: depends on project complexity

Sample approval: before production

Mass production: after sample approval and production lock
